# Projected Solid — Asymmetric Probe

Status: **internal generated-native research probe; no product promotion**

This source-independent probe starts from an unequal four-point profile with a
clipped corner and bowed edge. A deterministic generator displaces the profile
180 scene units along a 31° screen-space axis and emits ordinary editable front,
side, back, and source-facing edge paths. One visible Source Optics owner drives
regional front and side response.

## Inspect it

1. Open `projected-solid-probe` and fit the artboard. The silhouette must read as
   one projected payload without motion or a camera.
2. Select the front, side, back, and edge carriers in Layers. Each is ordinary
   editable Scene geometry with native paint.
3. Select **Single material source** and inspect its two Source Optics response
   targets. No global look owns the depth read.
4. Use Direct Select on **Front — source profile snapshot**. Direct edits remain
   local to that ordinary node; sibling faces do not regenerate live.
5. Save/reopen, duplicate, or delete carriers as normal nodes. This proves native
   output behavior, not a coupled projected-solid authoring contract.

The generator recomputes all roles when its frozen profile, axis, or depth input
changes. The editor currently has no durable live owner that performs that same
recomputation after direct edits. The probe therefore keeps the product decision
at `no-promotion` while making the missing contract precise. Its frozen fit and
100% edge packet received the independent critic verdict `request-user-review`:
the asymmetry, face order, and source-relative edge pass automatic rejectors,
while the user must decide whether it reads as one projected solid or two joined
panels. No public tutorial/gallery entry or aesthetic acceptance is inferred.
