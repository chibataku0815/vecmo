# Signal Handoff

Status: **internal integration proof; revised object language awaits user visual review**

Signal Handoff is the source-independent integration proof for the completed
core-motion loops. One horizontal pressure passes through five native owners:
exact affine motion parenting, exact custom temporal curves, a partial-channel
transform constraint, a nested same-artboard traveling matte, and ordered path
morph correspondence.

The visual carriers now share one circle-cell language: a sparse constrained
wake compresses toward the aperture, and its cell vocabulary reappears as a
fully occupied masked signal field with one connected two-row accent band. The
aperture remains the only non-circular changing form because its four-point
correspondence is the load-bearing morph proof. This replaces the superseded
solid line/rectangle treatment without changing the motion-capability chain.

## Inspect it

1. Open the internal `signal-handoff` reference scene and play the 90-frame phrase.
2. Select **Inherited wake** to inspect its Position/Rotation constraint and
   fixed source offset.
3. Select a controller key, expand Timeline mode, and enable **Graph** to compare
   Value and Speed.
4. Select **Main signal field** to inspect the red-channel matte driven by the
   nested **Changing aperture** source.
5. At frames 0, 52, or 76, use Direct Select on **Changing aperture** to inspect
   adjacent-key correspondence, preview a seam/winding change, or repair counts.

The sample remains internal rather than becoming a public preset. Its technical
delivery QA passed on 2026-07-13. A later user-directed object-quality revision
superseded the original solid primitive treatment; final aesthetic acceptance of
the revised pixels remains user-owned. Public tutorial promotion is a separate
product decision.
